# Organic brand growth roadmap
Date: 8 October 2026. Status: proposed strategy, not a completed campaign.

## Positioning
- Hemayet Hossain: Salesforce and AI solutions engineering supported by accurately labelled project evidence; Forward Deployed Engineering is a direction until demonstrated.
- Hossain Consulting: practical CRM and automation for Sydney trade/home-service businesses, initially enquiry handling and quote follow-up. Test this positioning against real customer questions, recruiter interest and search data.
- Keep hemayethossain.com and hossainconsulting.com separate by audience; preserve portfolio.hossainconsulting.com as the evidence hub.

## Permanent operating rules
Never remove either website footer or its existing links. Use current social URLs from src/lib/site.ts and live footers. Trailblazer identifies the founder.
Use organic growth only. Keep paid ads, boosts, purchased backlinks, advertising pixels and retargeting inactive. Future advertising needs separate explicit approval; traffic levels alone do not authorize it.
Prepare exact content and named destinations for approval before publishing websites, social posts or directory listings. Keep personal phone and home address private. Never invent clients, results, credentials, testimonials or project completion.
A skill or scheduled drafting task is not platform authentication or permission for unattended publication.

## Priorities
1. Publish original project demonstrations, engineering decisions, verification and lessons.
2. Create topic clusters linking articles, project evidence, relevant services and useful related content. Use natural cross-site links.
3. Align identities, public bios, URLs and truthful status across platforms.
4. Earn relevant mentions through useful resources, genuine participation and eligible directories.
5. Measure search clicks, relevant referrals, recruiter conversations, enquiries and bookings alongside engagement. Do not invent volumes, rankings or traffic forecasts.

## 90-day sequence
| Period | Deliverables | Review gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–14 | Verify profile identity and links; inspect indexing and crawler access; record available baseline metrics; identify priority audience questions | Approve positioning and first exact content pack |
| Days 15–45 | One substantive evidence topic per week where supported; distinct personal/agency article drafts; three adapted posts and a demonstration plan | Review factual claims, assets, links and named accounts before publication |
| Days 46–90 | Improve topics attracting relevant interest; create useful resources; prepare suitable listings and collaborations | Review measured outcomes and approve specific submissions/publications |

Do not produce filler when project evidence is insufficient. Reuse the existing Monday morning Australia/Sydney blog-and-social draft routine instead of creating duplicate schedules.

## Efficient content pack
Include source commit/tag, verified facts and limitations, distinct article drafts, final channel copy, image/alt text or labelled brief, video/captions/transcript/thumbnail or labelled unrendered script, destinations, proposed schedule and validation evidence. Prefer actual approved screen recordings and honest workflow demonstrations. Distinguish prepared, rendered, approved, scheduled, live and indexed states; verify public URLs after approved publication.

## Search and AI discovery
Make content accessible, original, clear and attributable. Check canonical URLs, crawl controls, sitemap ownership, metadata, internal links and actual indexing. A 200 response is not proof of indexing.
- ChatGPT: assess OAI-SearchBot access separately from training controls. Eligibility is not guaranteed inclusion.
- Claude: assess documented search and user-fetch crawler controls using current official guidance.
- Google generative search: ordinary SEO and useful original evidence remain foundations. Do not promise AI citations or treat AEO/GEO as a guaranteed mechanism.
- Grok: maintain useful public web/X content; search capabilities do not create a guaranteed recommendation route.
- Periodically test neutral audience questions and record date, model/search mode and citations. Label these observations as samples, not population-wide visibility measurements.

## Tools
Use Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, native platform reports and GitHub first. Inspect actual account access before promising operations.
Use Ubersuggest's limited free account only as optional keyword research, not a recommendation engine. Do not start paid trials or subscriptions.
Use one AI assistant for drafting and another for selective claim review; verify claims against source evidence. Do not assume API use, video rendering or open-source hosting is free.
Keep external scheduler installation a separate decision. Postiz and marketing-skills were researched as related repositories, not installed.

## Reuse for future engagements
Pilot on the two owner brands and document actual outcomes. For recruiter contracts and client businesses, establish a separate audience, brand brief, verified facts, account permissions, approval contact, scope and reporting baseline. Never mix customer data or publish client content using the owner's accounts. Footer preservation is an owner-brand rule; obtain each client's own site requirements. Establish engagement terms separately.
