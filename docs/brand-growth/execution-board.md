## Useful additions

1. Accessibility and mobile quality: keyboard navigation, readable contrast, descriptive alt text, captions/transcripts and responsive layouts.
2. Search hygiene: broken internal links, www/apex redirects, correct host-specific canonicals, crawl errors and sitemap processing.
3. Performance: measure Core Web Vitals and image/page weight; diagnose actual bottlenecks before adding optimisation tools.
4. Trust: visible author, evidence-backed project status, clear services/scope, contact email, privacy and terms. Preserve simulated and AI-assisted labels.
5. Conversion measurement: relevant enquiries and bookings alongside search clicks/impressions. Begin with available search-engine reports; do not install tracking until its purpose and privacy controls are defined.
6. Publishing discipline: maintain one source article per audience, adapt channel copy, check links/media, record public URLs and refresh dated claims. Avoid generic bulk AI posts.
7. Account continuity: owner retains ownership, uses official sign-in/MFA, reviews collaborator scopes and keeps recovery arrangements private.

Additional search engines are a later review, not a reason to delay Google/Bing. Evaluate supported submission methods and IndexNow where useful; IndexNow does not submit routine blog URLs to Google. There is no special AEO registration that guarantees an AI assistant will cite a site.
