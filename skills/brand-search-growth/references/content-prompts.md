# Content prompts

Adapted from the owner's saved 26 September 2026 SEO playbook. Current website constants and verified candidate facts override older examples, including booking duration.

## Personal article

Write for a hiring manager about [topic]. Evidence supplied: [repository, dated checks, screenshots]. Current project status: [simulated/in progress/completed]. Explain the problem, approach, actual implementation, verification, limitations and next learning step. Distinguish AI-assisted work. Start with a direct answer and use readable headings. Provide title, summary, related project links and recruitment next step. Do not invent first-person experiences to fill gaps. Length follows the useful evidence, not an SEO word quota.

## Agency article

Answer [business owner's question] in plain Australian English. Describe the process before software. Include a checklist or clearly labelled example, limitations, contact preferences and an online discovery next step. Do not invent clients, metrics, guarantees or legal approval. Link current sources for regulated or factual claims. Keep marketing consent separate from a requested service response.

## Fair comparison

Compare [vendors] using their own pages read on [date]. Table: use case, current pricing/currency/tax, usage limits, integrations, setup, support and sources. Mark anything unavailable as unknown. Distinguish vendor claims from independently verified facts. Do not infer ratings, performance, data residency or certifications. Disclose affiliate relationships where applicable.

## Visual and short video

From [approved article], create one five-stage infographic with exact copy, accessible contrast and no invented numbers; one six-slide carousel; a 30-second script with captions; and channel-specific posts. Retain demo labels and customer privacy. Include a link to the owning site. Deliver files and distinguish prepared content from platform publication.
