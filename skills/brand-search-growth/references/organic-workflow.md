# Organic approval and distribution workflow

## Approval pack

For each topic include audience, source commit/tag, verified facts, simulation/planned labels, article draft, final platform copy, destinations, image with alt text or labelled brief, rendered video or labelled script, captions/transcript, asset rights, proposed schedule and validation evidence. Clearly distinguish a rendered asset from a production plan. Request one approval for the specific batch and named accounts; do not broaden it to future posts.

## Efficient production

Batch one useful topic into three adapted posts: a practical answer, a visual explanation and a demonstrated lesson. Treat cadence as a hypothesis; prefer fewer strong posts over filler. Use a source ledger to avoid repeated articles. Link personal channels to personal content and agency channels to agency content. Preserve existing footer navigation.

For faceless video use approved screen recordings, cursor highlights, short captions and narration or a silent caption-led edit. Show simulation labels in the footage. Redact secrets and personal/customer data. Use no fabricated results, unlicensed footage or impersonated voices. Export only when an actual rendering pipeline is available and inspect the final file.

Use native scheduling after approval where supported. Check current platform link behavior: YouTube Shorts description URLs generally are not clickable, so use suitable channel-profile or related-video paths. Do not assume one aspect ratio, audio licence or caption fits every platform.

## Listing checklist

Check directory relevance, public listing URL, duplicates, current eligibility, free versus paid tier, contact fields and privacy. Prepare consistent truthful details; publish no home address or personal mobile. Submit only the approved listing and verify its resulting URL. Do not treat submission as provider acceptance or a guaranteed backlink.

## Measurement

Begin with available Search Console, Bing and platform reports. Record the time window, source, impressions, clicks, relevant landing-page referrals, enquiries, saves and shares where available. Use campaign links without sensitive identifiers. Do not install analytics, pixels or retargeting as part of content preparation. Review after a meaningful data window and avoid invented benchmarks.
