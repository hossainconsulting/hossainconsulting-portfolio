## Listing decision

### Recommended review candidate: Brownbook

Brownbook is the most submission-ready candidate found tonight, but it is **prepared, not approved or submitted**.

Evidence checked:

- Brownbook currently advertises a [free listing, free claim and free enhanced profile](https://www.brownbook.net/).
- Its [free-listing page](https://www.brownbook.net/free-listing) says the free entry can include keywords and a website link.
- In the [current add-business form](https://www.brownbook.net/add-business), only business name, business category and country are marked required. Address, city, postcode, phone, mobile and fax are not marked required. The form supports email, website, blog and social links.
- Its [terms](https://www.brownbook.net/terms-conditions) require a genuine business and an authorised representative. They also state that submitted data becomes Brownbook property and that registered users authorise Brownbook and third parties to market related products and services. The terms prohibit automated/robot-driven use outside the official API.

Privacy fit: the home address, personal mobile and a fabricated business number can all be omitted from the visible first-step form. The marketing/data clauses are a material owner decision, so no account was created and no form was submitted.

Duplicate check: a public web search on 9 October found no exact Brownbook result for `Hossain Consulting` in Sydney. This is not proof that no duplicate exists. Brownbook's own form instructs users to search first, and its terms restrict automated use, so the exact in-site search must be performed manually after approval.

Category fit is still unverified because the public text snapshot did not expose the dropdown values. Select only a truthful consulting, CRM, IT or business-services category visible in the live form; do not force an unrelated category.
